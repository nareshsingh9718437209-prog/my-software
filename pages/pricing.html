Questions, answered

How does auto-renewal work?

Monthly, quarterly, and yearly subscription plans renew automatically at the end of the billing period unless cancelled. You can manage or cancel renewal from your account or by contacting support.

Can I upgrade or switch to a lifetime plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade your plan or move to a lifetime license. Our team can help you choose the best option based on your current subscription and usage.

Is GST included in the price?

No. Prices shown exclude 18% GST. Applicable GST will be added at checkout and a GST invoice will be provided.

What does multi user mean?

Multi-user plans allow multiple users/systems to access DotERP as per the selected plan limits and setup (for example, with DotERP Server for team environments).

What is your refund policy?

Refund and cancellation terms depend on the plan and activation status. Please contact our sales/support team for detailed policy information before purchase.
